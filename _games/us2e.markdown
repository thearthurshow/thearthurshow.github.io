    <a href="https://magpiegames.com/collections/urban-shadows" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><i>Urban Shadows</i></a> is an urban fantasy tabletop roleplaying game in which mortals and monsters vie for control of a modern-day city in a political battleground layered just under the reality we think we know. The streets bleed shadows as the supernatural politics of the city threaten to swallow you whole.

    Will you suffer as the savior for those who don't have enough, or take advantage of the weakness in friend and foe alike? Will you fight the darkness…or give in to power?
