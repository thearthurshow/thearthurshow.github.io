About

I fell in love with collaborative storytelling by LARPing at the ripe old age of 8, and I want to bring that love to your table. Using my exhaustive background in stage improv, long nights reading system rulebooks, and over 10 years of GM experience, I will lead gameplay that fits your group's play style. Let's craft a story together, one driven by the characters you create, that you'll remember for years to come.

How would you describe your Game Master (GM) style?

Roleplay focused, improvisational, enthusiastic, a safe space for players. Encouraging exploration, and players to be a part of the world building process.

What makes you unique as a GM?

I create a story around the players characters, taking every bit of information they give me and making it a central part of the story, rewarding player engagement with a deeper connection to the world.

What else should people know about you?

I prioritize my table to be a safe space for all who sit at it, roleplaying can be a vulnerable act and I am here to help make everyone have a good time. I'm not here to push your boundaries, but create a space where you feel comfortable exploring your own.

What apps or software do you use to run your games?

Discord, Google Meet, Roll20, Tabletop Simulator
