Arthur is an incredible GM. My current game that he is leading is my first in years and he has really made it easy for me to take part. Even more than that, he deftly merges players of lower and higher experience so that we all have fun. I love his NPC voices and roleplay as well as the inclusion of visuals and music. He takes a video chat session and makes it engaging. He is also quite good at incorporating player wishes into his plans and very flexible with the progression of the story. You will not regret hiring Arthur!

- Sean
